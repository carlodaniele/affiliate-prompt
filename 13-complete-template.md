## How to Use This Template

### Step 1: Copy the Full Prompt

Copy the complete template from [affiliate-content-prompt-template.md](./prompt/affiliate-content-prompt-template.md)

### Step 2: Customize

Fill in the bracketed sections with your specific information:

- `[industry, product category, and relevant topics]` → Your actual industry
- `[general audience]` → Your actual reader
- `[content type]` → Product review, comparison, how-to, etc.
- Other bracketed placeholders → Your specific context

### Step 3: Use with Your LLM

Paste the customized prompt into:

- ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or your preferred LLM
- Include the CONTEXTUAL GROUNDING section with your actual product data
- Add your TASK INSTRUCTIONS for the specific assignment
- Run the prompt

### Step 4: Review and Refine

The model will output content following your template. Review it against:

- Your editorial principles
- The REASONING CRITERIA checklist
- Your TASK REQUIREMENTS

If the output falls short, adjust the template and try again.

## Customization Quick-Start

### For a Product Review

1. Open the template
2. Fill in PERSONA with your editorial voice
3. Fill in AUDIENCE with target readers
4. Set FUNNEL stage to BOFU
5. Add CONTEXTUAL GROUNDING with product information
6. Set TASK INSTRUCTIONS to "product review"

### For a Comparison Guide

1. Open the template
2. Follow steps 1–4 above
3. Set FUNNEL stage to MOFU
4. Add CONTEXTUAL GROUNDING for all products
5. Set TASK INSTRUCTIONS to "comparison"

## Key Takeaways

This template solves the core problem of affiliate content:

✓ **Grounds claims in evidence** (not vendor marketing)
✓ **Separates editorial judgment from monetization**
✓ **Prioritizes reader value** (useful independent of clicks)
✓ **Makes constraints explicit** (hard rules, not guidelines)
✓ **Provides validation** (reasoning criteria checklist)
✓ **Demonstrates quality** (few-shot examples)
✓ **Ensures consistency** (output schema)
