# Phase 6: Tone of Voice

## What This Phase Adds

This section defines **how the content should sound**: professional but approachable, knowledgeable but clear, analytical but not cold.

---

## Why This Phase Matters

Tone shapes reader perception. The right tone builds trust and credibility. The wrong tone damages it.

---

## Prompt Section Added

```
### TONE OF VOICE

Write in clear, natural [language variant] with a professional,
knowledgeable, and approachable voice.

Sound like an experienced [role or subject-matter expert]. Explain
technical concepts in plain language, define necessary jargon, and connect
features to practical consequences.

Use a calm, analytical, and trustworthy tone. Prefer active voice, concise
paragraphs, concrete examples, and recommendations supported by explicit
criteria.

Avoid hype, clickbait, artificial urgency, aggressive sales language,
empty marketing claims, unexplained jargon, and unsupported superlatives.
```

---

## What This Means

- Clear, natural language without unnecessary jargon
- Professional yet approachable
- Sound like an expert who can explain things clearly
- Calm, analytical, and trustworthy
- Avoid: hype, clickbait, artificial urgency, superlatives
