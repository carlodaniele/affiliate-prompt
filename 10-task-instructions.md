# Phase 10: Task Instructions

## What This Phase Adds

This section defines **exactly what you want the model to produce**: content type, structure, length, required sections, and specific requirements.

---

## Why This Phase Matters

Without task instructions, the model guesses. With them, the model knows exactly what to deliver.

---

## Prompt Section Added

```
## TASK INSTRUCTIONS

Create [content type] about [provider, product, service, or comparison].

Funnel stage:
[TOFU / MOFU / BOFU].

Primary search intent:
[informational / commercial investigation / transactional / navigational /
troubleshooting].

Primary query:
[main search query].

Specific target audience:
[describe the audience for this article].

Editorial angle:
[describe the perspective, problem, or decision the content must address].

The content must:

- [required point or section];
- [required point or section];
- [comparison, testing, or recommendation requirement];
- [pricing, limitations, or evidence requirement];
- [CTA requirement].

Do not introduce competitors or comparisons unless they are explicitly
required and supported by the contextual grounding.

Before writing, identify missing, outdated, contradictory, or unsupported
information. Do not fill information gaps with assumptions.
```

---

## What to Specify

- **Content type:** Product review? Comparison? How-to? Buying guide?
- **Funnel stage:** TOFU, MOFU, or BOFU?
- **Primary search intent:** What does the reader want?
- **Primary query:** The main search keyword
- **Target audience:** Who is this for?
- **Editorial angle:** What problem does it solve?
- **Requirements:** Pricing table? Comparison? Specific sections? Evidence requirements?
- **Don't include:** What should NOT be in the content?
